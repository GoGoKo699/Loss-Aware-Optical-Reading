A concerns the complete joint optimum with a normalized input before unequal
loss, arbitrary final measurements, and erasure accounting. It is not simply
the known decoder for a fixed ensemble. The [R1-R3 comparison](audits/novelty-01/REDUCTIONS.md)
identifies the model-specific input/filter normalization reduction. The inspected
follow-up sources do not directly subsume that complete contract. This leaves a
candidate, not worldwide priority clearance.

The one-bad-path zero-error comparison remains supporting work under checkpoint-06
restrictions. Preserve its high-energy non-nulling branch in the all-source proof.
KKT allocation, norm perturbations, Jensen, fixed-N concentration, and numerical
root enclosure are supporting methods, not separate physical discoveries.

## Resolved access questions and remaining limits

**S18:** Huang, Lami, Singh, Wilde, [2603.19911v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19911v1).
Relevant main results and appendices were inspected, including Appendix F's
finite-n statement. It is binary asymmetric, reference-assisted discrimination,
not the same multiclass one-photon C/E/F contract. Its Fock-diagonal signal
marginal does not authorize dropping a purification; its specific coherent
comparison does not optimize every classical receiver. No direct subsumption of
A or the complete optical certificate was established by this comparison.

**S20:** the original theorem and proof were recovered from embedded author-uploaded
article text, with publisher metadata cross-checked. The original-text gap is
resolved. A publisher PDF download, visual PDF check, or bytewise publisher-copy
identity is not claimed.

S24 (a 2022 minimum-error bound) and S25 (a 2002 strengthened inconclusive bound)
remain partial discovery leads at the recorded access levels. Neither is used
to infer theorem absence or exact equivalence. S22 already establishes the
reclassification independently of those gaps. The task does not exhaust all
citation networks, theses, or unpublished work.

A separate supplied 717-assertion follow-up and the committed 572-check audit
reach the same conclusion by differently recorded reductions. The former is
preserved separately in [integration evidence](integrations/novelty-02/REPORT.md);
its original directory name does not overwrite the latter. The earlier 631
checks and both newer suites test identities/examples, not priority.

## Hardware and statistics

The laboratory questions are not an equipment inventory. A universal mode mesh,
as in Clements et al., [1603.08788](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08788), does not itself
establish the source, hidden-operation interface, shared-phase calibration, or
available classical receiver. Initial [lab feedback](experiment/LAB_REVIEW.md)
precedes execution.

The trial certificate is fixed-N under the stated independent or conditional
energy contract. No optional-stopping, total-apparatus-energy, adaptive repeated-
query, or wall-clock advantage is imported. Historical audits and source maps
remain unchanged in their archives; this page updates the current interpretation.
