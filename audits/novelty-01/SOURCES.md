# Primary sources and actual inspection scope

This is a bounded comparison of specific statements. Full-text access means the identified passages were read, not that every theorem and appendix was re-proved. Versions and locators refer to the retrieved editions. Papers screened only by abstract or task definition are not used to certify the absence of a theorem. No third-party article files are redistributed.

## S01. Guha and Shapiro

*Capacity of optical reading, Part 1: Reading boundless error-free bits using a single photon*. arXiv:1207.6435v3; PRA 87, 062306 (2013).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1207.6435v3).

**Locator:** Eqs. (7)-(8), single-photon Hadamard receiver discussion; loss discussion in Sec. IV.  
**Inspection:** Targeted full HTML passages read.  
**Consequence:** The physical phase-code/interference mechanism and its photon-budget motivation are inherited. Convert their per-pixel illumination to our total signal budget. This is not itself an unequal-loss, finite-error joint-design theorem.

## S02. Chefles and Barnett

*Optimum Unambiguous Discrimination Between Linearly Independent Symmetric States*. arXiv:quant-ph/9807023v1 (1998).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9807023).

**Locator:** Sec. III, Eq. (3.15).  
**Inspection:** Targeted full PDF passages read.  
**Consequence:** The minimum-coefficient USD result specializes directly to cyclic Fourier-code survivors. A standard Gram argument covers the remaining flat codes. Inverse-loss normalization is a short corollary.

## S03. Chefles, Kitagawa, Takeoka, Sasaki and Twamley

*Unambiguous discrimination among oracle operators*. arXiv:quant-ph/0702245; J. Phys. A 40, 10183 (2007).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0702245).

**Locator:** Sec. V, Eqs. (5.17)-(5.20).  
**Inspection:** Targeted full PDF passages read.

## S04. Eldar and Forney

*On Quantum Detection and the Square-Root Measurement*. arXiv:quant-ph/0005132v2 (2000).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0005132).

**Locator:** Sec. 8.3, constant diagonal of the square root of the weighted Gram matrix; PDF p. 34.  
**Inspection:** Targeted full PDF passages read; decisive page screenshot inspected.  
**Consequence:** The fixed input code decoder satisfies the known SRM optimality condition, even without cyclic group symmetry. This removes a separate decoder-novelty claim, not the joint input/frontier statement.

## S05. Hashimoto, A. Hayashi, M. Hayashi and Horibe

*Unitary-process discrimination with error margin*. arXiv:0912.2610v3; PRA 81, 062327 (2010).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0912.2610).

**Locator:** Sec. II, Eqs. (1)-(7); scope of the Secs. III-IV solutions.  
**Inspection:** Targeted full PDF passages read.  
**Consequence:** Joint input/POVM error-margin discrimination is not a new task. The displayed solved cases are unitary two-process and finite-group settings, not this general unequal pure-loss code contract.

## S06. Nair and Yen

## S08. Bagan, Munoz-Tapia, Olivares-Renteria and Bergou

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.4145).

## S09. Primaatmaja, Ho and Scarani

*Optimal single-shot discrimination of optical modes*. arXiv:2012.11104; PRA 103, 052410 (2021).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11104).

**Locator:** Sec. II, energy/phase-reference model and Eqs. (3)-(15); Sec. V, Coda: on losses, Eqs. (43)-(44); Sec. VI.  
**Inspection:** Full-text PDF recovered; formal access model and losses coda inspected.  
**Consequence:** The work is not simply lossless: it treats mode-independent loss and discusses changed preferred probes. ## S10. Chuan-Wei Zhang, Chuan-Feng Li and Guang-Can Guo

*General Strategies for Discrimination of Quantum States*. arXiv:quant-ph/9908001v1; Phys. Lett. A 261, 25 (1999).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9908001).

**Locator:** Eqs. (3)-(5), (7), (9), (13).  
**Inspection:** Targeted full PDF passages read.  
**Consequence:** Gram feasibility and the coexistence of error and failure are established. The explicit binary overlap/error relation is a predecessor to the binary statistics bound; it is not an optical all-source multiclass statement.

## S11. Bouchet, Rachbauer, Rotter, Mosk and Bossy

*Optimal control of coherent light scattering for binary decision problems*. arXiv:2108.03755v2; PRL 127, 253902 (2021).

[Primary record or full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2108.03755v2).

**Locator:** Eqs. (1)-(3), discrimination operator and maximal-eigenvector input.  
**Inspection:** Targeted full HTML passages read.

## S17. Kaiyuan Ji and Bartosz Regula

*Beyond Hoeffding and Chernoff: Trading conclusiveness for advantages in quantum hypothesis testing*. arXiv:2510.07601v2 (9 December 2025).

## Retrieval and interpretation cautions

Herzog, Bagan, and Primaatmaja full PDFs were recovered despite earlier unsuccessful HTML retrievals. Bouchet, Nair-Yen, and unitary error-margin predecessors were added beyond the original short bibliography. The full loss discussion in Primaatmaja must not be omitted merely because an earlier abstract-level reading missed it.

Search sometimes returned a publication-like snippet attached to a generic arXiv, APS, or index home page. Such results were used only as discovery leads and not as theorem evidence. A guessed arXiv identifier 2010.15783 resolved to an unrelated article and was rejected; the Zhuang source is 2010.15753.

Publication/version timestamps embedded in rendered HTML can disagree; the explicit arXiv version is the source identifier. The 2024 Melo paper is labelled by its arXiv version, not by a later date appearing in an HTML header.

No claim in this audit says that every result not located in these passages is new. In particular S18 needs a full follow-up comparison before a claim of comprehensive current bosonic-literature coverage, and S20 has the stated full-text gap.
