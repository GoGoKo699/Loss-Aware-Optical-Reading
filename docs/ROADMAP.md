# Research and laboratory roadmap

The handover now provides separate routes for the laboratory's confirmed SU4
and SU8 processors. Begin with [commissioning](../experiment/COMMISSIONING.md)
and choose the [SU4](../experiment/SU4.md) or [SU8](../experiment/SU8.md) recipe.
No procurement or acquisition is authorized by this roadmap.

## Established scope

A, complete joint input-only retuning under the exact flat-code assumptions,
remains the leading candidate contribution. B remains a derived optical
source-class benchmark. The fixed-input decoder, zero-error endpoint and
benchmark's generic discrimination ingredients are credited. The completed
source comparisons retain their resolved access qualifications; broader priority
and publication significance remain open.

The four-mode [robustness study](ROBUSTNESS_GUIDE.md) is supplied, reproduced and
integrated, with original work-order/evidence history preserved. It gives exact
structured results and certified finite examples, not a solution for arbitrary
noisy meshes or a laboratory calibration. The native eight-mode Walsh recipe
specializes the existing photon theorem without transferring the four-symbol
classical frontier or four-mode robustness claims.

Use the [uncertainty worksheet](../experiment/UNCERTAINTY.md). Missing laboratory
values are inputs to future adaptation, not a reason to leave the handover
unfinished. No laboratory message or hardware execution occurs in this task.

## Extensions that are not established

Exact arbitrary-loss classical finite-error optimality; a native-eight-mode
source-class advantage experiment; native-eight-mode robustness; arbitrary-noise
fixed-receiver optimality; adaptive repeated-query or total-dose advantages;
occupied bypass rails, idlers, quantum memory and interacting ancillary photons
all require separate scoped work and any necessary proofs. Eight single-path
flips are not the orthogonal four-label task scaled up.

The useful next research choice should be driven by the laboratory's actual
error structure. Do not reopen completed source tasks or add an apparatus merely
to increase mode count. Review the [claim ledger](../CLAIM_STATUS.md) and
[current task](../work_orders/CURRENT.md) before further changes.
