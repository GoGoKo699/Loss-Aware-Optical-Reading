# M1: change the input, keep the decoder fixed

**Question:** as the permitted error penalty changes, does the predicted input
retuning explain the measured correct, wrong and inconclusive rates with the
same receiver? Use the available SU(4) chip or ports 0–3 of the available SU(8)
chip. This is the existing single-photon mechanism test, not an independent
quantum-advantage certificate or an experimental proof of global optimality.

The controlling acquisition rules are preserved in
[FIRST_EXPERIMENT.md](FIRST_EXPERIMENT.md). Begin with
[commissioning](COMMISSIONING.md). Required additional capabilities are a
single-photon source model at P0, independent hidden phase/loss control, a
separate receiver role, complete output recording, and pre-exposure trial
timing. These supporting interfaces and their calibrations have not been supplied;
both processors themselves are confirmed available.

## Exact four-label task and preparations

Logical ports are ordered `0,1,2,3`, matrices have output rows/input columns,
and hidden labels are independent uniform draws in that same order. At P0,
prepare `|a> = sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i>`. Between P0 and P1 implement

$$
O_j=I_4-2|j\rangle\langle j|,\qquad
T=\mathrm{diag}(\sqrt{\eta_0},\ldots,\sqrt{\eta_3}).
$$

After P1 keep `D4=J4/2-I4` fixed. A single click on `k` answers `k`; no click
or multiple clicks is inconclusive. SU8 uses `a ⊕ 0`, `O_j ⊕ I4`,
`T ⊕ I4`, and `D4 ⊕ I4`, with inputs 4–7 in vacuum and every spare-output
detection retained as failure. See the explicit matrices, preparation unitary,
common SU phase, and port tables on [SU4](SU4.md) and [SU8](SU8.md).

The illustrative loss vector is `eta=(.7,.7,.7,.14)`. It is not a lab
specification. Compare four preselected preparations:

| Preparation | Exact rule | Illustrative weak-path probability `p_3` |
|---|---|---|
| Uniform | `p_i=1/4` | .25 |
| Zero-error | `p_i=(1/eta_i)/sum_k(1/eta_k)` | .625 |
| Penalty 1 | Positive normalized top eigenvector `z` of `B_1`, `p_i=z_i^2` | About .193814 |
| Penalty 5 | Positive normalized top eigenvector `z` of `B_5`, `p_i=z_i^2` | About .457125 |

$$
B_\lambda=\frac{1+\lambda}{4}vv^T-\lambda\,\mathrm{diag}(\eta),
\qquad v_i=\sqrt{\eta_i}.
$$

These are input-only settings, computed classically before testing. For each
one, the ideal model predicts

$$
s=\sum_i\eta_ip_i,\qquad
C=\frac{(\sum_i\sqrt{\eta_ip_i})^2}{4},\quad E=s-C,\quad F=1-s.
$$

At the illustrative zero-error point, `C=.35, E=0, F=.65`. Balancing the
surviving amplitudes suppresses wrong answers while many attempts remain
inconclusive. At finite penalty the best input need not put the most possible
weight on the weakest path.

## Procedure and fixed controls

1. Identify the physical blocks and P0/P1 using [interfaces](INTERFACES.md).
   Verify the logical mode map and common optical phase during commissioning.
2. Calibrate the actual losses and model deviations using separate calibration
   and pilot records. Compute all four input files from that pretest record.
   Do not impose the example losses as required specifications.
3. Freeze the decoder target, output rule and each condition's configuration
   identifiers. Calibration may adjust settings to realize the same target
   before the test; it may not train a new receiver on held-out outcomes.
4. Choose the condition schedule and attempt count before acquisition. The
   preserved protocol suggests about 20,000 attempts per non-primary
   characterization condition, subject to a pretest plan. M1 has no new
   uncorrected primary significance claim.
5. For each counted attempt, draw a fresh independent uniform hidden label,
   allow the independently specified settling interval, and expose one gated
   probe. Preparation and receiver do not receive the current label. An upstream
   clock or pre-exposure herald defines the trial; detection does not.
6. Retain all attempts, seal the optical records, then unseal labels and compute
   each condition's unconditional confusion/mask table and `C,E,F`. Preserve
   separate pilot and test records. Do not stop after a favorable partial plot.

Retain trial ID, condition/preparation/receiver/calibration/code IDs, label for
offline scoring, full click mask, timing, energy-bound reference and integrity
flags. SU4 retains all 16 masks; the SU8 embedding retains all 256 before
four-label adaptation. No-click, multiple-click, leakage and rejected exposed
records remain attempts. Pre-exposure aborted cycles go in a separate ledger
and must not send uncounted light through P0.

## What a favorable result means

Agreement of the full unconditional rates with the predicted preparations
supports the input-retuning mechanism. The theorem, not a finite preparation
scan, supplies the optimum over all allowed inputs and measurements. A visible
gain over one baseline is not yet an advantage over all classical illumination;
that is the separately registered [P1](P1.md) test.

Unexpected errors can arise from misordered ports, relative phase error,
non-diagonal/distributed loss, a drifted receiver or incomplete source/readout
accounting. Information loss and regret from retaining the fixed decoder are
different questions. Review [uncertainty inputs](UNCERTAINTY.md), the
[robustness guide](../docs/ROBUSTNESS_GUIDE.md), and the
[study calibration worksheet](../studies/robustness-01/CALIBRATION_WORKSHEET.md).
The [theory route](../docs/THEORY_ROUTE.md) leads to
[Theorems 1–2 and their proofs](../proofs/THEORY.md).
