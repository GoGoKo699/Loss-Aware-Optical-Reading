# P2: an implemented coherent reader above the photon bound

**Question:** in severe unequal loss, can a declared coherent illumination and
an actually implemented receiver exceed the upper score of the entire permitted
four-path one-photon class? Use the available SU(4) chip or four active modes
of the available SU(8) chip as preparation/routing hardware. P2 uses a coherent
displacement-and-click receiver; this is a separate receiver from the fixed
photon decoder used in M1/P1.

The registered meaning and acquisition rules remain in
[FIRST_EXPERIMENT.md](FIRST_EXPERIMENT.md). Both chips are available. Supporting
capabilities still to confirm include a compatible coherent source/reference,
couplers and stable displacement at the outputs, an independent hidden section,
P0 energy calibration, common-phase maps, gated readout and complete records.
If displacement is unavailable, P2 remains a theoretical negative region;
direct laser intensity measurements are not this reverse comparison.

## Exact illumination, hidden task and receiver

Logical ports and labels are `0,1,2,3`, with output rows/input columns.
Between the incident signal plane P0 and accessible device-output plane P1,
the task remains

$$
O_j=I_4-2|j\rangle\langle j|,\qquad
T=\mathrm{diag}(\sqrt{\eta_0},\ldots,\sqrt{\eta_3}).
$$

Use fresh independent uniform hidden labels, unknown to both illumination and
receiver. The illustrative severe-loss point is
`eta=(.7,.7,.7,.035)` and the penalty is `lambda=5`.

For the preserved hypothetical receiver example, use incident coherent
amplitudes and energies

$$
\alpha=(\sqrt{.33},\sqrt{.33},\sqrt{.33},0)^T,
\qquad n_i=|\alpha_i|^2,
\qquad \sum_i n_i=.99.
$$

A unitary preparation with first column `alpha/sqrt(.99)` maps coherent
amplitude `sqrt(.99)` from input port 0 to this vector; the exact completion is
given on [SU4](SU4.md). These values are a worked example, not a mandated lab
illumination. The calibrated upper mean budget in the example is one.

After P1, apply the **same displacement `-T alpha` for every label**. In the
canonical common phase convention, the remaining amplitude is

$$
b_j=T O_j\alpha-T\alpha=-2\sqrt{\eta_j}\alpha_j|j\rangle.
$$

Use direct output detection, with identity mode routing rather than `D4`.
Displacement is an affine optical operation implemented with a coherent
reference and coupling; it is not an SU matrix and is not furnished by
programming the interferometer alone. A physical common phase multiplier `c`
on every hidden `O_j` requires the matched displacement `-c T alpha`, or an
explicit common phase compensation. Do not null a differently phased reference.
No setting may use the current hidden label.

On SU8 embed the preparation and `O_j,T` in ports 0–3, with inputs 4–7 in
vacuum. Use identity eight-mode routing for this receiver and displacement
`(-T alpha) ⊕ 0`. Monitor the spare outputs and count leakage as failure.
The matched common phase is `c=exp(i*pi/8)` if that SU8 hidden representative
is physically used. See [SU8 route A](SU8.md#route-a-four-active-modes) and
[interfaces](INTERFACES.md) for the port/phase map and alternatives to cascading
the chips.

With ideal nulling and ideal detectors, only the marked populated path can
click. Its conditional click probability is `1-exp(-4 eta_j n_j)`.
Thus this implementation has `C=sum_j[1-exp(-4 eta_j n_j)]/4`, `E=0`, and
`F=1-C`. Since the weak-path energy is zero in this example, label 3 produces
an inconclusive outcome, not a secretly removed trial. The lab receiver's
coupling loss and imperfect nulling remain in its achieved rates.

## Procedure and records

1. Commission ports, preparation and actual complex maps. Identify P0, P1 and
   the receiver displacement plane in the [interface record](INTERFACES.md).
   Determine whether compatible reference/coupling and output monitoring exist.
2. Using calibration/pilot records, set an illumination and fixed displacement
   independent of the test labels. Measure/bound coherent signal mean at P0,
   including uncertainty and tails. Account for reference power separately;
   the signal budget is not total apparatus energy.
3. Supply the photon-class exclusion bound for the actual declared channel.
   For nominal diagonal losses and a simultaneous map radius, use the proven
   perturbation allowance and outward-safe photon support endpoint. Do not
   replace it by the score of one tested photon preparation.
4. Freeze illumination, displacement, port decision, calibration IDs, penalty,
   N and confidence plan before the held-out data. The preserved provisional
   template has **N=50,000**, `lambda=5`, and `alpha_stat=.00025`.
   Any pilot-based revision precedes held-out outcomes.
5. Draw fresh independent uniform labels for single-use gates, wait for the
   specified settling interval and keep the reader blinded. An upstream
   clock/pre-exposure source event defines an attempt. Acquire the fixed N,
   seal optical records, then unseal labels and score.
6. One active click reports its port; no click, multiple click, leakage or a
   predeclared rejected exposed record is inconclusive. Preserve every attempt
   and every mask. Do not condition on clicks, stop on significance, or remove
   unfavorable records. Pre-exposure aborts have their own no-exposure ledger.

Raw records include trial ID, condition/calibration/code/preparation/receiver
IDs, sealed label, all detector masks/time tags, gate and source timing,
energy-bound reference, displacement configuration ID and integrity flags.
SU4 retains 16 masks and SU8 retains 256; the latter uses explicit four-label
adaptation with spare detections mapped to failure, never deleted.

## Bound and quantitative example

For the whole permitted one-photon/four-path architecture, the conservative
score ceiling is

$$
U_{\rm ph}=\beta_{q,U}(5;\eta_U)+2(1+5)\epsilon,
$$

where `beta_q,U` is the numerical support upper endpoint and `epsilon` is the
justified simultaneous map-deviation radius for the declared model. At the
illustrative losses the nominal ideal support is `.1860224979`. With example
radius `.003`, the conservative ceiling is about `.2220224979`. Receiver loss
in our coherent implementation reduces our measured score; it does not lower
this unrestricted photon receiver bound.

The registered statistical radius is `6 sqrt(log(1/alpha_stat)/(2N))`.
At N=50,000 it is about `.05464300`. A measured score lower bound above
`U_ph` supports the reverse conclusion. The program-wide allocations remain
`.0005` for simultaneous calibration failures plus `.00025` each for P1 and
P2 statistics, at most `.001` together under their stated assumptions.

The unchanged hypothetical model adds combined post-device survival/detection
`.92`, dark probability `1e-6` per detector per fixed gate, residual background
intensity `1e-4` per output, and independent Gaussian path-phase sigma `.035`
rad. It predicts approximately `C=.42909091, E=.000520743, F=.57038835`,
score `.42648719`. These are planning outputs, not calibrated hardware
performance, experimental data, or guaranteed statistical power.

## What a favorable result means

An implemented coherent score above the valid whole-class photon ceiling
demonstrates a regime where that classical strategy beats every allowed
one-photon preparation and measurement in the four tested paths. It does not
beat every quantum architecture, idler-assisted probe, occupied reference rail,
multi-query protocol, or multiphoton input. Those are outside this task.

Wrong phase matching in the nulling reference, answer-dependent displacement,
unaccounted reference light crossing P0, an unsupported map radius, incomplete
leakage records, or comparison against one poor photon preparation would break
the intended interpretation. Consult [uncertainty inputs](UNCERTAINTY.md),
[theory route](../docs/THEORY_ROUTE.md),
[the photon-class negative-comparison proof](../proofs/THEORY.md),
[numerical contract](../docs/NUMERICAL_CONTRACT.md), and
[robustness guide](../docs/ROBUSTNESS_GUIDE.md).
